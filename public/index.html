🎯 The Objective

Two rival spymasters know the secret identities of 25 agents. Teammates must guess all their team's cards (Red or Blue) before the opposing team, while avoiding the deadly Assassin card!

👥 Roles & Claiming

  • Red & Blue Spymasters: See the secret 5x5 color key grid. Give 1-word clues + number of related cards (e.g., "Ocean 2").
  • Field Operatives: Only see word cards. Discuss as a team, tap cards to guess, and decide when to pass turn.
  • Spymaster Lock: Players claim Spymaster in the lobby before the match begins.

🔄 Turn Flow

  1. Spymaster submits 1 clue word and 1 number representing the number of cards related to the clue.
  2. Operatives discuss and tap cards one at a time:
    • ✅ Team Card: Scores +1 point. Team can keep guessing (up to Clue + 1) or tap Pass Turn.
    • ⚪ Bystander (Neutral): Turn ends immediately.
    • ❌ Opposing Card: Turn ends immediately and gives the opposing team a free point!
    • ☠️ Assassin (Black Card): INSTANT LOSS! The team that taps the Assassin loses the game immediately (unless Soft Assassin rule is enabled).

⚙️ Customizable House Rules (Host Settings)

  • Starting Advantage: Random (9 vs 8 cards), Equal (8 vs 8 cards), or Team-specific start.
  • Guess Limit: Clue + 1 (Standard catch-up rule), Strict (Clue number only), or Unlimited.
  • Assassin Mode: Instant Loss (Standard tournament) or Soft (Lose point and pass turn).
  • Turn Timer: Set 60s, 90s, 120s, or Unlimited turn durations.

🏆 Winning

The first team to reveal all their secret agent cards wins the match! (Or wins if the opposing team taps the Assassin).

🎯 The Objective

  • Innocents: Unmask the hidden Imposter(s) through subtle questioning before time runs out.
  • The Imposter: Blend in, deduce the secret location from dialogue, or guess the location without getting caught!

🎭 Secret Roles & Peeking

  • Innocents: See the exact Category and Secret Location (e.g., Category: Landmarks, Location: Eiffel Tower).
  • The Imposter: Knows they are the spy, but the location is secret (❓ SECRET).
  • Privacy Peek Card: Tap & Hold the secret card to peek your role privately away from friends sitting next to you.

🔄 Game Phases & Flow

  1. Discussion Phase (Timed): Ask each other questions in person (e.g., "Is it hot here?", "Do you pay to enter?"). Don't make questions too obvious or the spy will figure out the location! Host can add +30s or skip.
  2. Cover-Typing Phase (Optional):
    • Enabled (20s): Everyone types on their screen simultaneously. Innocents type a verification word; Imposters can submit a location guess or type PASS to avoid sound/touch giveaways.
    • Disabled: Skips typing and proceeds straight to voting.
  3. Secret Voting Ballot: Cast secret votes on who you suspect is the Imposter. Once enough eliminations occur, living players can vote "No Imposters Left" to trigger a game check.
  4. Results Tally: The player with the most votes is ousted (identities remain hidden until the game concludes).

🏆 Winning

  • Innocents Win: All Imposters are eliminated, or an Imposter makes an incorrect location guess, or town unanimously votes "No Imposters Left".
  • Imposters Win: An Imposter correctly guesses the secret location, or living Imposters equal living Innocents ($\ge$).

🎯 The Objective

  • Civilians & Town: Find and eliminate all Murderers through night investigations and daytime voting.
  • Mafia / Murderers: Eliminate townspeople until Murderers equal or outnumber living civilians.

🎭 Secret Roles

  • 👑 Host (Narrator): Runs the game, reads narration prompts aloud, sees all roles in God-mode, and reviews the Secret Action Ledger.
  • 🔪 Murderers (1–3): Secretly choose a victim each night. If split, swap votes live on-screen to reach consensus.
  • 💉 Doctor (1): Protects 1 player each night (can protect self). Rule: Cannot protect the same person two rounds in a row.
  • 🔍 Detective (1): Investigates 1 suspect each night. Receives immediate feedback: 🔴 Murderer or 🟢 Not Murderer. Maintains a personal investigation ledger.
  • 😇 Civilians: Sleep at night; debate and vote during the day.

🔄 Round Flow

  1. 🌙 Night 1 (Murderers): Murderers wake up silently and agree on a victim.
  2. 🌙 Night 2 (Doctor): Doctor selects someone to protect. If it matches the victim, the victim survives!
  3. 🌙 Night 3 (Detective): Detective investigates 1 living suspect.
  4. 🌅 Morning Report: Host announces whether someone was murdered or saved.
  5. 💬 Town Discussion: Timed in-person debate (Host can add +30s or skip).
  6. 🗳️ Secret Town Ballot: Secret voting ballot with live anonymous vote counts and an Abstain / Skip option.
  7. 📊 Results Tally: Strict majority ($\lfloor \frac{\text{living}}{2} \rfloor + 1$) eliminates a suspect. If no majority is reached, no one is eliminated.

🏆 Winning

  • Civilians Win: When all Murderers are eliminated!
  • Mafia Wins: When living Murderers equal or outnumber living Civilians ($\ge$).
